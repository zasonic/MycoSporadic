## Overview and zones
- Overview shows three demo zones, environmental chart, and activity.
- Mist, FAE, and Heat toggles change the active-output count.
- Open a zone, change target bands, and close with Done.
- All outputs off requires confirmation and sets active outputs to zero.
- Temperature, Humidity, CO₂, time ranges, and zone filters change the chart.

## Planner
- Parts planner shows $519.22 for three zones and three loads at Tier 1.
- Changing quantities or tier recalculates the total.
- Search filters rows; no-match state offers Clear search.

## Commissioning and safety
- Commissioning checkboxes update completion and can be unchecked.
- Safety guide and connection details open and close without navigation loss.

## Firmware
- Firmware zone selection changes the YAML preview.
- Export YAML downloads the selected zone configuration.

## Responsive navigation
- Desktop navigation switches among all five views.
- Mobile menu opens, navigates, and closes at 375px.
